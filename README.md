# 📊 Simple IRPF Tax Return Organizer

Structured spreadsheet system designed to centralize and manage personal data, bank balances, and financial documents for annual income tax declarations.

---

## 🚀 About the Project
A tool developed to simplify the preparation of personal income tax returns (IRPF), preventing document loss and facilitating the cross-referencing of financial information with tax authority software.

---

## Project Structure 

* **`TITULAR`**: Complete personal and registration data for the individual (CPF, address, contact details, and dependent/foreign resident status).
* **`INFORMES`**: Bank account management, current balances, and links to PDF income statements.
* **`NOTAS`**: Monthly records of revenue inflows and pay slips.
* **`TABELAS`**: Official list of bank codes and financial institutions within the National Financial System (SFN).
